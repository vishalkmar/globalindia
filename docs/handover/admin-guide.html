1. Signing in and your security

  1. Open /admin, enter your email and password, and press Continue.
  2. Enter the 6-digit code from your authenticator app, or the code emailed to you. You can paste it.
  3. The first time, the site asks you to choose a new password and set up a second step (authenticator app or email code), and shows 10 recovery codes. Print them or keep them somewhere safe and offline. Each works once if you lose your phone.
Passwords need at least 12 characters. Passwords that appear in known data leaks are refused. A long sentence you can remember works well.

Things that keep the admin safe (and why you may see them)

My security (bottom of the menu): change password, sign-in email, authenticator, create new recovery codes, see and end your active sessions.

2. The dashboard

Shows enquiries today and this week, new enquiries waiting, product and page counts, recent edits, a 12-week enquiry chart, the most-asked products, and how many images still need a description (alt text). Aim to keep "images without alt text" at zero; it helps Google and visitors using screen readers.

3. Enquiries: answering buyers

  1. The bell at the top shows new enquiries. Enquiries lists them all; spam is kept under the Spam filter so nothing is lost.
  2. Open an enquiry to see the products, quantities and contact details. Set the status (New → In progress → Quoted → Won / Lost / Closed), assign it to a team member, and add internal notes (customers never see notes).
  3. Export downloads the current filtered list as a spreadsheet. Exports are recorded in the audit log because they contain personal data.
Settings → Enquiries and spam: who receives new-enquiry emails, the automatic "we received your enquiry" reply, blocked emails, domains and words, and the option that editors see only enquiries assigned to them.

4. Products and categories

Add or edit a product

  1. Products → New product. Fill name, category, CAS number (checked automatically), grade, purity, form, pack sizes, minimum order and dispatch time.
  2. Add the buyer's guide sections you need: specifications, applications, storage, documents, FAQs. Empty sections are hidden on the website.
  3. Add photos from Media and documents (TDS, SDS, COA). Mark a document public only if anyone may download it; batch COAs stay private and are sent with quotes.
  4. Fill the SEO panel (title up to ~60 characters, description ~150). Leave empty to use the defaults.
  5. Save as draft to keep working; Publish to show it. Changing a slug (web address) automatically creates a redirect from the old one.

Categories work the same way and group products into the six families shown on the site. Import and export of products as a spreadsheet are on the Products page.

5. Pages and sections

Home, About, Why us, Quality, Gallery, Careers, Downloads, legal pages and the Delhi / Delhi NCR pages are built from sections (hero, text, product families, FAQ, call-to-action and more).

  1. Open a page, use Edit on a section, change the text or image, and Apply changes.
  2. Reorder with the arrows, hide a section with Hide, or add a new one.
  3. Save draft keeps changes private. Preview opens the page as visitors will see it. Publish makes it live. Older versions can be restored from History.

6. Industries, articles, FAQ, gallery

  • Industries: one page per industry, linked to the products it uses.
  • Insights: articles. Set a future publish date to schedule one.
  • FAQ, Testimonials, Client logos: ordered lists. Only add testimonials and logos you have permission to show.
  • Gallery: photos with categories; visitors can filter and open them.

7. Photos and documents (Media)

  • Drag files onto Media or use Upload. Allowed: JPG, PNG, WebP, AVIF (up to 5 MB) and PDF (up to 10 MB). SVG and PDFs with scripts are refused for safety.
  • Always write alt text: one short sentence describing the photo ("50 kg bags of soda ash on a pallet").
  • Set the focal point so crops keep the important part. Replace swaps a file everywhere it is used.
  • A file in use cannot be deleted until it is removed from the pages that use it; the media detail shows where.

9. Team members and roles

RoleCan
ViewerRead everything in the admin, change nothing.
EditorAdd, edit, publish and delete content, answer enquiries, prepare themes.
Super adminEverything, plus users, security settings, theme going live, audit log, permanent delete.

Users → Invite user sends a link to set a password; the new person sets up their own second step. Changing a role or disabling someone signs them out immediately. Keep at least two super admins so you are never locked out.

10. Redirects, emails, trash, audit log

  • Redirects: send an old address to a new page (301 permanent). The 145 addresses of the old website are already redirected; the hit counts show which ones are still used.
  • Emails: preview every automatic email and send yourself a test.
  • Trash: deleted items stay 30 days and can be restored; then they are removed automatically.
  • Audit log: who did what and when, including sign-ins, exports and refused actions.

11. When something goes wrong

ProblemDo this
Lost phone with the authenticatorSign in with a recovery code, then set up the app again in My security.
Forgot passwordForgot your password? on the sign-in page; the link works once for 15 minutes and signs out every device.
A change does not show on the siteCheck it was Published (not only saved as draft). Wait a minute, then Settings → Clear website cache.
Strange sign-in email or activityChange your password, use Sign out everywhere, check the Audit log, and tell your developer.
Site attacked or very slowFollow the "Under Attack" steps in the security runbook (docs/security/27.md) or call your developer.
