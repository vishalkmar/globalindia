### How ordering works
1. Send your enquiry — product, grade, quantity, pack size and delivery city.
2. Get a written quote — usually the same working day, with sample if needed.
3. Confirm and pay — material is packed, labelled and dispatched.
4. Receive with documents — invoice, COA and SDS where applicable.
